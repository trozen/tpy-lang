The `tpy.dispatch` stand-in under CPython runs the first variant applicable
by arity and type (it does not check element types such as `list[int64]`
and `Iterable[int]`). Every pick here goes against that: `floor_b`,
`concrete_first`, `scalar32`, `scalar_big`, `to_big` and
`twin_generic_first` take a candidate declared after one that would widen
the list, `tiers` takes its generic variant over a concrete one that
widens. The same rules in orders the stand-in follows live in
`calls/overload_pending_container`.
