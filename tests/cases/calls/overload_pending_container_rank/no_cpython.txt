The `tpy.dispatch` stand-in under CPython tries concrete variants before
generic ones and otherwise picks by declaration order (it skips
generic-alias annotations such as `list[int64]` and `Iterable[int]`). Every
pick here goes against that: `floor_b`, `concrete_first`, `scalar32`,
`scalar_big` and `to_big` take a candidate declared after one that would
widen the list, and `tiers` takes its generic variant over a concrete one
that widens. The same rules in orders the stand-in follows live in
`calls/overload_pending_container`.
