The rebind-slot split is currently unconditional (`rvalue_reassigned_vars`
alone), so a superseded value survives in the init slot until function exit
instead of dropping at the rebind the way CPython does. Tracked in BUGS.md
("gated on whether the init value is actually aliased") -- once the slot is
alias-gated this case is expected to reach CPython parity and lose this marker.

CPython also does not auto-chain __del__ to a base class, which test_inherit
exercises separately.
