The cpy phase cannot run this case, and cannot be made to.

It dies on the first line: `import _bindings`. The raw @native binding
layers under lib/tpy are internal to the library path and have no lib/cpy
stub, by design -- they declare C++ symbols, not Python.

Do NOT "fix" that by stubbing _bindings and putting lib/tpy on PYTHONPATH.
31 of the 88 import names here (os, io, json, re, socket, ssl, http, math,
time, urllib, collections, itertools, ...) are also real CPython stdlib
modules, so CPython would resolve them to its OWN implementations and the
phase would report a green while testing none of TPy's library.
