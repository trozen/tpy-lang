Arc method auto-deref (`data.lock()` resolving through Arc's Deref[Mutex[T]]
to Mutex.lock(), no explicit `.get()`) is a compile-time deref-chain feature
the CPython Arc stub does not model -- same as tests/cases/tplib/rc_auto_deref.
This case exists specifically to exercise that TPy-only auto-deref, so it can't
run under CPython (`data.lock()` raises AttributeError on the stub Arc).
